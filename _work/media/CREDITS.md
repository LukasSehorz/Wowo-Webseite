WebM: `libvpx-vp9 -crf <x264 CRF + 8> -b:v 0 -deadline good -cpu-used 4 -an` (three noisy clips use CRF 38/42 so that the WebM stays smaller than the MP4). ## Gaps (nothing usable found under a free commercial license)

- **P4 insoles / orthotics in a hand or in a shoe** – the free libraries only offer sneaker product shots on white, shoemaker workshops or prosthetics. This needs the client's own product photos (the generated `insole-in-hands.jpg` / `product-*.png` currently fill this slot).
- **Inserting an insole into a shoe** (video) – not available.
- **Therapist assessing a foot in a clinical setting / real gait analysis with measuring equipment** (video) – only massage/spa clips, a private-setting foot palpation and gym treadmills exist; the three `physio-*` clips are the closest honest matches.
- **Handing over a voucher in a workplace** – not available without the cheesy handshake/thumbs-up look; `team-conversation.jpg` and `work-production-line.jpg` cover the employer page instead.
- **Retail staff shown anonymously** (from behind / legs only) in good quality – not found; `tile-handel.jpg` shows a recognisable person.
- Everything depicts anonymous stock models, not the founders, their practice or the football club – a short own shoot (hands fitting an insole, the thermoforming step, the pitch-side physio bag) would lift the site more than any further stock search.
